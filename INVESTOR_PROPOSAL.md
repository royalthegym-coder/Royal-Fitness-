## 3. Business Model: The SaaS Revolution (Revenue Engine)

This system operates on a **Multi-Tenant SaaS (Software as a Service)** model designed for rapid global scalability.

* **Tiers of Monetization:**
  1. **Starter (Single Gym):** Core admissions, attendance, and WhatsApp automation for independent gyms.
  2. **Enterprise (Chains):** Global Admin Dashboard and AI Vision for multi-branch fitness chains.
  3. **Elite (White-Label):** Custom-branded mobile and web apps with the gym owner's own name and logo.
* **Revenue Streams:** Monthly subscription fees + payment gateway transaction commissions + premium AI health reports (Upselling).

Our infrastructure enforces **ISO-Standard Privacy** and cryptographic security:

* **Auto-Destruct Policy:** Automatic ephemeral scrubbing of raw medical uploads after AI processing.
* **Quantum-Resistant Auth:** Biometric FIDO2 Passkey login (FaceID/Fingerprint) eliminating password vulnerabilities.
